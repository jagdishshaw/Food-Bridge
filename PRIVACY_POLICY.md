# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2025

FoodBridge (“we”, “our”, “us”) values your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 1. Data we collect
- **Account information:** name, email, phone number, role (donor, NGO, admin).
- **Location data:** address, latitude/longitude for pickup and map features.
- **Listing details:** food description, quantity, photos, safe‑until time.
- **Usage data:** IP address, device type, browser, analytics cookies.

## 2. How we use your data
- To enable food‑matching between donors and NGOs.
- To send transactional emails (listing posted, claim, collection confirmation).
- To generate impact statistics displayed publicly.
- For security, fraud‑prevention and account verification.

## 3. Legal basis
We process data based on your consent (e.g., signing up) and legitimate interests (prevent waste, safety).

## 4. Data retention
- **Accounts:** retained until you request deletion.
- **Listings:** automatically removed after the safe‑until time or when marked *Collected*.
- **Logs:** retained for 90 days for audit purposes.

## 5. Your rights
- Access, correct, or delete your data via the *Account Settings* page.
- Export a copy of your data.
- Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

## 6. Third‑party services
We use Supabase (hosting, auth, storage) and Resend (email). Both adhere to GDPR‑compatible terms.

## 7. International transfers
Data is stored on servers located in the United States and the European Union. We employ standard contractual clauses.

## 8. Contact
If you have questions, email privacy@foodbridge.org.
